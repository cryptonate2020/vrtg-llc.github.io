About VRTG

VRTG

VRTG is a veteran-owned IT consulting firm headquartered in Cape Coral, Florida. We bring over 300 combined years of technology expertise to every engagement, spanning network infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud platforms, and unified communications.

Founded in 2022, VRTG was built to deliver the depth of a large SI with the responsiveness of a boutique firm. Our consultants are practitioners first: engineers who've designed and deployed the systems they now advise on.

We focus on commercial clients who need trusted, long-term technical partners. No junior resources on senior problems. No generic recommendations. Just experienced engineers doing the work.
